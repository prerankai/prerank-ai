Frequently asked questions

How does the Idea Score work?

PreRank estimates demand, competition and evergreen value for your topic and niche, then combines them into one 0–100 score with a GO / PROMISING / RISKY verdict. Scores are PreRank's own transparent estimates, clearly labelled — not copied from any other tool.

Are the thumbnails really AI-generated?

Yes. The Thumbnail Generator creates original AI artwork from your report or your own prompt (up to 5,000 words), with an optional reference image, and adds a CTR analysis for every design.

What do I get for free?

The Free plan includes 3 reports and 3 AI thumbnail generations per month, 1 channel with audit, the Feed and the Keyword Explorer. Pro removes the tight limits.

Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in; you keep your saved reports either way.
